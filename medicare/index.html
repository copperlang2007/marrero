Medicare pathways

Two different structures. Different trade-offs.

Plan details vary by area, carrier and individual circumstances. Use this as education, not a plan recommendation.

Medicare Advantage

Private-plan alternative to Original Medicare

  • Includes Medicare Part A and Part B services.
  • Plans may include additional benefits.
  • Provider networks and cost-sharing rules may apply.
  • Prescription drug coverage may be included in some plans.
Original Medicare

Federal Medicare coverage

  • Includes Part A and Part B.
  • Beneficiaries can generally use providers that accept Medicare.
  • Separate Part D coverage may be purchased.
  • Some people consider Medicare Supplement coverage for additional cost protection.
