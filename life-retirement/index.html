Life insurance

Protection for the people and obligations that continue after you.

Marrero Group’s current materials describe term, whole life and universal life insurance as part of its protection offering.

Term

Temporary protection for a defined period.

Whole Life

Permanent coverage when policy requirements are met.

Universal Life

Permanent coverage with flexible features that vary by contract.

Questions to bring

Who depends on your income? What obligations would remain?

Use those answers to frame the protection conversation before discussing a product.
