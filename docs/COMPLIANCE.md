# COMPLIANCE

## Rules

- Do not invent regulated claims, eligibility outcomes, licensing facts, carrier relationships, endorsements, or government affiliation.
- Keep Medicare information educational unless an approved workflow explicitly supports more.
- Preserve the statement that Marrero Group is not connected with or endorsed by the U.S. Government or federal Medicare program where Medicare content appears.
- Do not represent the email inquiry form as secure enrollment.
- Do not solicit sensitive enrollment information through email.

## Content sources

Business facts and testimonials must come from approved Marrero materials or verified public sources. Unknown facts are marked unverified rather than inferred.

## Change control

Compliance-sensitive copy changes require explicit source verification before release.
